<p class="hero-roles">I build <span id="typed-role" class="typed-role">browser automations</span><span class="typed-cursor">_</span></p>

I'm Tushar, a business analyst and computer science engineer in Melbourne, Australia, working across AI, ML and automation. I hold a Master of Data Science from Monash University (July 2025) and a Bachelor of Computer Science and Engineering from North South University in Dhaka.

I work at Gaura Travel (also trading as Global Travel Xperts and iFlynow), where I joined in May 2026 as an intern and now work part-time. My title is Data Analyst, but day to day I work across business analysis, development and AI engineering: I gather requirements from sales, operations and HR, then design and build the systems that meet them. My work sits where data meets automation. I designed the four-layer architecture behind the company's automation ecosystem: **Python** with **Playwright** for browser automation, **FastAPI** services managed by **PM2**, **n8n** for orchestration, and **PHP/MySQL** for storage and dashboards. It covers airline ticketing, payments, reconciliation, stock management, email ingestion, HR and rostering, and reporting. See the [projects](/projects/) page for how the pieces fit together.

Before that, I was a Research Assistant and Teaching Assistant at North South University, where I published a first-author paper at BMVC 2022, and I led data work on GigNestMel, a Monash capstone product for gig workers in Melbourne that earned a Distinction. Today I'm bringing that machine learning background into day-to-day work, fine-tuning small self-hosted language models that classify customer needs from call transcripts.

Outside work I self-host n8n on a home NAS behind Tailscale, write small desktop tools in Rust, and read a lot about databases and typography. My [cv](/cv/) has the details, and the [notes](/blog/) section holds the occasional write-up.
