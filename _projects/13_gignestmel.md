GigNestMel was my Monash capstone project (February to July 2025), built by a team of five in an Agile environment. It helps food delivery and ride-share drivers in Melbourne with their day-to-day work and gives them safety and peace of mind. Live site: [gignest-mel.pages.dev](https://gignest-mel.pages.dev/home).

## My role

Data Analyst and Engineer, with some wider IT work.

- Researched candidate datasets against the requirements, then tested them for integrity and usability.
- Prepared the datasets for use in the product.
- Ran data quality testing and validation to keep the system reliable.
- Wrote technical documentation and user guides for the project deliverables.
- Contributed to front end and back end work, server configuration and SQL optimisation.

## Outcome

We delivered features shaped by user and stakeholder requirements, including a map tailored to gig workers, a safety section and a weather section, and presented the work at the capstone showcase. The project earned a **Distinction**.

## Stack

R (data analysis), Python (visualisation), MySQL.
